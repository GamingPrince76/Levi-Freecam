# ⚡ Levi Freecam

## BREAK FREE. LEAVE YOUR BODY. EXPLORE EVERYTHING.

A standalone native Freecam mod for Minecraft Bedrock on Android.

Built for LeviLauncher & LeviLaunchroid.

---

## 👁️ SEE BEYOND YOUR PLAYER

Levi Freecam lets you detach your camera and explore the Minecraft world from a different perspective, without normally moving your player away from their original position.

No ordinary perspective. No ordinary exploration.

Just you, your camera, and the world beyond your character.

## 🚀 FEATURES

- 👻 Detached Camera — Explore the world independently of your player.
- 🕹️ Free Movement — Move the camera using directional controls.
- ⬆️ Vertical Movement — Move the camera up and down.
- 📍 Player Position Preservation — Keep your player at their original position while Freecam is active.
- 🛡️ Packet Blocking — Block selected gameplay packets while Freecam is enabled.
- 📦 Standalone Native Mod — Distributed as a ".levipack" for compatible Android launcher setups.

## ⚙️ INSTALLATION

### Ready to break the limits?

1. Download the ".levipack" file from the repository's Releases section.
2. Import the pack into a compatible LeviLauncher or LeviLaunchroid setup.
3. Launch Minecraft Bedrock and enable the mod.
4. Enter a world and test your Freecam controls.
